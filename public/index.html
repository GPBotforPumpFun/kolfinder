Publication evidence

Live Europe PMC searches of PubMed-indexed records, restricted to disease terms in titles and abstracts and the chosen date window. Expert queries use surname and initials. Name matches are candidates and can include other people with the same name. Review author affiliations and ORCID before relying on a match.

Europe PMC documentation ↗

Collaboration, not inferred influence

Edges indicate shared authorship in the retrieved publication sample. Counts reflect this sample, not complete career output. Citation counts belong to papers and are not an expert influence score. Map locations use recognized institution names in reported affiliations; unknown institutions remain unmapped.

Discovery methodology

Candidate authors are ordered by appearances in the 100 most recent matching publications, then by citations to those sampled papers. This is a literature signal, not a definitive KOL ranking. All authors, including consortium members and researchers, can appear. Identity, specialty and clinical role require review.

Trial context

ClinicalTrials.gov results match the condition plus an optional expert-name text search. A name appearing in a registry record does not establish investigator status. Review the original record. Trial totals are matching registry records, not confirmed trials led by this person.

Starter directory

A small selection of recognizable research names with reference links, not an exhaustive or certified roster. Institution and city are starting context and may change. Historical or uncertain affiliations are labeled. Open Payments, grants, congress roles, contact details and your private KOL data are not connected.

Your workspace

Notes and saved profiles stay in this browser's local storage. There are no user accounts, shared teams or secure CRM records in this version. Avoid entering sensitive patient information. Exported briefs include sources, retrieval time and research limitations.
